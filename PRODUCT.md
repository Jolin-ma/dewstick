## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary visitor:** someone who already spends on skincare-adjacent beauty — lip masks, sleeping masks, treatment balms in the roughly $18–35 range — and would consider a tinted version if it did not feel like a compromise on either care or color. They arrive on a phone, usually from an Instagram or TikTok post, and have never heard of Dewstick before the moment they land. The homepage has to explain the whole idea within one screen of scrolling and make the shades one tap away.

**Secondary audience:** Simplified-Chinese readers. Every string on the site ships in English and 中文, switchable; this is a first-class requirement, not a localization afterthought.

**The person running the case study** (the site's true audience in practice): this is a portfolio artifact. A reviewer of the work should be able to see the whole e-commerce coordination workflow — product sourcing, storefront, funnel, results — reflected in how the site and its supporting decisions are built.

## Product Purpose

Dewstick is a **fictional product** used to run a real, self-directed case study in end-to-end e-commerce coordination. The workflow the project demonstrates, in order:

1. Scan live marketplaces for a viable product to sell.
2. Stand up the website / store.
3. Design the content-to-purchase funnel.
4. Read the results — conversion by traffic source, etc.

The site is a **store**: a real Shopify development store (`dewstick.myshopify.com`) with a working test-mode checkout, plus a public static copy of it on Vercel (`dewstick.vercel.app`) for the portfolio, since a Shopify development store can't be made public. The earlier pre-launch waitlist page was retired and deleted on 2026-10-23.

Because the product is fictional, invented specifics (pricing, shade names) are legitimate as long as they stay plausible and internally consistent — but the *funnel logic, the analytics story, and the "how would this actually run" details matter as much as visual polish.*

## Positioning

**Confirmed lane: luxury lip treatment first, color is the bonus.** Dewstick is "a lip mask that happens to have color" — skincare-first, warm, and a little more elevated than the playful, value-priced space occupied by the close analog tuttilipstick.com. Tutti sells nearly the same idea (lipstick that acts like a balm), the same ingredient trio, a similar cloud/blob packaging pattern, and a similar bold rounded wordmark. Dewstick deliberately does **not** sit in that lane: the differentiation a neighbor could not truthfully copy is the "luxury lip mask, in a stick" treatment framing — squalane, shea butter, and ceramides doing the work of a $30+ lip mask, with real color layered over it, positioned and art-directed as skincare rather than as a novelty lipstick.

Voice follows from this: warm and simple, not joke-forward. A skincare brand that happens to be fun, not a bit.

## Operating Context

- **Arrival:** almost entirely mobile, one-handed, from a social post. No pinch-zoom should ever be required (and it must never be disabled).
- **Decision window:** ~15 seconds. The visitor is cold and the page competes with the app they came from.
- **Funnel:** homepage → shade → add to cart → checkout (Shopify, test payment gateway — no real money moves). The newsletter ("Stay in the dew") is the secondary capture; on Shopify signups land in Customers tagged `newsletter` plus the landing URL's UTM source / medium / campaign. On the Vercel copy, cart and newsletter are demos and say so.
- **Design reference:** the 2026-10-23 redesign follows the editorial structure of susannekaufmann.com (study and decisions in `docs/redesign/DESIGN.md`; as-built system in `DESIGN.md`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Website (Vercel):** static pages generated by `scripts/build_shop.py` — home, collection, both products, cart, 404, in English (`/`) and 中文 (`/zh`). Stylesheet `shop/dewstick.css`, scripts `shop/site.js` (localStorage cart, menu, scroller). No framework, no backend; the repo root deploys on every push to `main`.
- **Copy:** the Shopify theme's `locales/*.json` plus `scripts/site_strings.json` (redesign copy, EN + 中文). Any new string ships in both languages.
- **Shopify theme:** `shopify-theme/`, a custom Online Store 2.0 theme. It still runs the previous design; porting the redesign is the next step.
- **Fonts:** Newsreader (headings, buttons) + Hanken Grotesk (body), Noto Serif SC / Noto Sans SC for 中文, Bodoni Moda for the wordmark.
- **Imagery:** the store's own photo set (`shop/img/dewstick-01…13`, square artwork with baked-in type — show whole, in square frames), the hero video (`shop/video/hero.mp4`), four ingredient photos (`shop/img/ing-*.jpg`) and the lips band image. Full-size originals live outside the repo in the owner's Pictures folder.
- **Product form:** twist-up bullet tube ("tinted lip balm"), 4g / 0.14 oz, sold as singles or a five-pack gift box ("happy shades happy days").
- **Confirmed pricing (canonical):** single **$22 CAD**, the set of five **$98 CAD** (compare-at $110, "save $12").
- **Confirmed shade names (canonical):** nectar (coral), poppy (true red), peony (rosy nude), blush (soft pink), orchid (berry rose). Homepage row: nectar, poppy, blush + the set.
- **Not yet wired:** real Instagram / TikTok URLs (the photo wall is deliberately unlinked — no real account exists); the "read the results" analytics layer (per-source landing → purchase / signup conversion).
- **Domain:** dewstick.com ownership/availability unconfirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** dewstick (lowercase); the wordmark is always Dew Red.
- **Existing lines to reuse verbatim or near-verbatim:** "half lipstick. half lip balm." · "all the care. all the color." · "one swipe. juicy glow." · "a luxury lip mask — in a stick." · "100% you" · descriptor tags: juicy, glossy, balmy · "asmr — the sound. the texture. the dew." · checklist: hydrating, tinted, effortless.
- **Ingredient story (fixed):** squalane (deep hydration), shea butter (soft & smooth), ceramides (strengthen & protect); framed as the benefit list hydrate / nourish / protect / glow.
- **Voice:** warm, simple, skincare-adjacent. Not joke-forward.
- **CTA wording:** "Shop the shades", "Find your shade", "Add to cart", "Join" (newsletter) — never "submit".
- **Newsletter reassurance line:** "New shades and news only. No spam, unsubscribe anytime."

## Evidence on Hand

- **Real:** the brand photo set (13 square images: five shade packshots with ingredient callouts, the set of five, unboxing, macro, lineup, pile, lips close-up, a get-ready-with-me model shot), the hero video, four ingredient photos, the redesign study (`docs/redesign/`), the archived waitlist build brief (`docs/archive/`).
- **Does not exist — future work must not fabricate it:** customer reviews, star ratings, testimonials, customer or order counts, organic UGC photography, real conversion data. The reviews slot is deliberately "No reviews yet." plus a founder note.

## Product Principles

1. **Shade first.** Every section earns the right to the next one; everything is judged against "does this help someone pick a shade and add it to cart on their phone".
2. **Treatment first, color is the bonus.** When a choice trades skincare credibility against lipstick-novelty charm, credibility wins. This is the whole reason Dewstick is not just another Tutti.
3. **The funnel is the deliverable.** Checkout, newsletter capture, traffic-source attribution and a believable "how this runs" story are core product, not polish to add later.
4. **Honest where proof is missing.** No fabricated reviews, counts, or UGC.
5. **Bilingual as a first-class constraint.** English and 中文 are equal; neither is a bolt-on.
6. **Stay lean.** Static pages, no framework, mobile-first, compressed imagery, no load-time popups.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Mobile: usable one-handed, no pinch-zoom required and never disabled; tap targets at least 44px on touch screens.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected (smooth scroll off).
- Language switch updates `<html lang>`; Chinese pages use `zh-CN`.
- Working standard: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and visible focus (2px ink outline, 4px offset).
